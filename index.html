The firm

Sixty-eight years on the same street.

Toh Theam Hock & Co was founded in Taiping in 1958 and has practised continuously from Perak ever since — through the transition to independence, the land reforms, and six decades of the state's property market.

That longevity is the firm's working advantage. Conveyancing in Perak is a matter of knowing the land offices, the developers and the bank officers personally. Files that stall elsewhere move here because someone at the firm has run the same transaction several hundred times before.

The practice today is led by four partners and spans conveyancing, banking and finance, corporate and commercial work, and family and estate matters. More than 50,000 clients have been served since the doors opened.

What we do

Practice areas

The firm's weight sits in property transactions and banking documentation, built over six decades of land office and bank panel work in northern Perak and Penang.

Conveyancing & real estate

Recognised for legal work across residential, commercial, office and industrial developments — from project inception through to perfection of title and charge.

  • Project conveyancing for residential, commercial and mixed-use developments
  • Management agreements, leases and tenancies
  • Sub-sale transactions and individual unit sales
  • Construction law, regulatory compliance and government policy alignment

Banking & finance

Acting for local and international banks and financial institutions in Malaysia on conventional and Islamic facilities, including high-value loans secured on foreign corporate securities.

  • Facility agreements and security documentation
  • Corporate, commercial, retail and housing facilities
  • Islamic banking products
  • Enforcement of security and debt recovery

Corporate & commercial

Advising local and international investors on establishing, structuring, maintaining and disposing of Malaysian businesses across a range of sectors.

  • Incorporation, capital reduction and corporate restructuring
  • Mergers and acquisitions of companies, businesses and undertakings
  • Shareholders' agreements and employee share option schemes
  • Licensing, franchising, agency, distributorship and joint ventures

Family law & estate planning

Succession work for families across northern Perak, from drafting a first will through to contested administration of an estate.

  • Wills and asset distribution
  • Gifts, trusts and estate planning
  • Probate, letters of administration and estate administration
  • Representation in estate-related disputes
